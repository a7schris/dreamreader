AI Statement

A note on AI, art, and actual humans.

Pillow talk from Misha

As an artist, I think it's important to be transparent about what's made by humans and what's made by AI. So, in the spirit of full disclosure, here's how The Dream Reader came to be and why…

First, I can confirm that I, Misha Collins, an actual human being, dreamed up this app. There is a certain read-by-the-author audio book that puts me to sleep almost instantly and I thought, if his boring voice can put me to sleep, perhaps, my boring voice will put you to sleep!

For a lot of us grown-ups, falling asleep can be hard, not getting enough sleep impacts our mental health, our physical health, our productivity, our social lives, our families—it impacts how our pets feel about us! It’s serious shit! So if people use this app and it works for them, they will be happier, healthier humans, and isn’t that something to strive for?!

I went into an actual recording studio, run by actual humans, and recorded my actual voice. I worked with a talented team of real people to develop the app, shape its design, and bring the whole thing to life. And I actually give a shit about whether you get a good night's sleep.

Where AI comes in

But we also used AI as a tool to replicate my voice. I don’t have time to read dozens of books aloud, but the books I used are either mine or in the public domain. Wherever I used an AI replica of my voice, it was at my direction and with my approval. This is me using my own vocal likeness to produce something that was my own creative inspiration. This is not someone stealing my voice to make something creepy in the world, nor is it me trying to trick the world into thinking I did something I did not.

I believe these tools can be incredibly useful, but I also believe we have to be careful and ethical in how we use them and be transparent when we do. That's a commitment we're making as we continue to develop The Dream Reader.

Another thing: Where an AI replica of my voice is used, we render the audio file once for thousands of users rather than generating it anew every time someone listens. So rest easy knowing that we didn’t have to build a new data center to get this app up and running.

If you have ever wanted to go to sleep with me, it’s finally going to happen now.

Thanks,

The real Misha.
